Retro Repair

Open it up. Find the fault. Bring it back to life.

An old console won’t power on. Bring it to your workbench, open the case, and find the fault. Clean, repair, or replace the part, then hit the power button and see whether it comes back to life.

Retro Repair is a hands-on 3D simulator about fixing old electronics for iPhone and iPad. Read the customer’s work order, pick the right tool, open the device piece by piece, and test it yourself before handing it back. Take on more jobs, order parts, personalize your workshop and revisit completed repairs in your album.

Start free with five starter devices. The optional Full Workshop purchase unlocks the rest of the device catalog and includes the mobile No Ads benefits. The game contains ads and optional in-app purchases. On iOS and Android, new parts orders take five minutes after your first four successful repairs; an optional rewarded ad or the separate No Ads + Instant Delivery purchase removes the wait.

Game languages: English and Turkish.
