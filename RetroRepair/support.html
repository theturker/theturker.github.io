Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems? Write to alperen997@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version and what happened.

Restoring the Full Workshop

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Saves

Your progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes the save. Active purchases can be restored with the same store account and an internet connection. iOS and Android purchases do not transfer between stores. Refunds or revocations remove the related access; repair progress and separately purchased items are preserved.
