Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Data on your device


No account is required. Progress, settings, album and poster images, an optional supporter name and purchase status are saved on your device.

Usage and crash data · iOS / Android


Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) is used to improve the game. Sent: which devices you open and repair, tutorial steps, parts orders, sales, unlocked products, rewarded ad result, language and app version; if the game crashes, a technical error report with device model and OS. Your name, e-mail, photos, supporter name and advertising ID are not sent, and this data is not used for ads. Firebase may process a random app instance ID and IP address. Where the law requires consent, collection waits for your privacy choice. Turn it off any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage data. Desktop versions send none. Google information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Steam · Mac / Windows


In the Steam version, achievements you earn (for example your first repair or how many devices you have repaired) are reported to Valve's Steam service and shown on your Steam profile. If Steam Cloud is on, your progress save is backed up to your Steam account; album and poster images are not. No other game data, name, e-mail or photos are sent. Without Steam running the game plays the same, offline. Valve privacy information: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement.

Optional ads · iOS / Android


You can choose a Google AdMob rewarded ad to accelerate parts delivery, double a job’s profit or take a VIP job. After repairs outside the tutorial, a full-screen interstitial ad may appear at most once every 3 minutes and up to 3 times per session. When an ad is prepared, Google UMP presents any required privacy choices; ads are requested only when permitted. Google advertising services may process IP address, coarse location, device/account identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. System and regional privacy choices apply. When required, change your choices in Settings → Ad privacy choices. The No Ads + Instant Delivery pack removes ads and delivery waiting. Desktop versions have no ads. Google privacy information: policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications


With your permission, local device notifications are scheduled for deliveries. After you allow notifications, occasional news and event notifications may be sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging; your device gets a random push token and joins a language group (TR/EN). Manage permission in system settings.

Photos and purchases


Poster images chosen through the system picker stay on your device. Apple or Google processes payments; the developer does not receive payment details. Product and transaction information and receipts are kept on your device to verify ownership. Purchases, restoration and ads require internet; repairs remain playable offline.
